Defects this run found, now fixed

LEAK Customers could see the referring partner's ID. The billing subscription and checkout responses included partner_id, coupon_id and pricing_rule_id inside the coupon block. Fixed with customer_view() in app/billing/subscriptions.py, used by every customer-facing billing response. Test: test_customer_billing_views_never_expose_the_referring_partner.

GAP Sign-ins made before approval weren't tied to the partner. An applicant's activity was logged without a partner ID, so the Super Admin's per-partner log missed it. approve_application() in app/partners/service.py now backfills those records when the partner is created.

Not covered by these checks

  • Razorpay is not connected to a live account. The probes use the internal confirm/renew path that a real payment webhook would call. Commissions follow invoices correctly, but a real payment hasn't been taken end to end.
  • Payout bank details are stored unencrypted. Only the partner and the Super Admin can read them, but they sit in the database as plain text.
  • Platform staff with partners.view can't open partner screens. The role matrix grants it to operations_admin, but all partner management lives under /api/super-admin/*, which is Super Admin only.
  • Email delivery itself isn't checked. The checks confirm messages are queued, not that they arrive.
